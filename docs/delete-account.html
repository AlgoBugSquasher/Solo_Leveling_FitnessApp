eXork Account Deletion

This page provides the external account deletion resource for the eXork fitness application.

Before You Continue

Account deletion permanently removes your eXork account and associated data after the applicable deletion process is completed.

eXork currently uses a deletion grace period. A user who signs back into the account during the grace period may cancel the pending deletion.
